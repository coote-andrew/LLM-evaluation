What it does

Cicada is the Clinical Informatics Centre's workbench for systematically testing prompts, models, and agents on gold-standard clinical data.

  1. Projects Upload labelled CSV or Excel with input and expected-output columns.
  2. Prompts Build reusable prompt templates with placeholders for your data fields.
  3. Models Configure LLM endpoints or register agents to call during evaluation.
  4. Runs Execute prompts against models asynchronously and inspect every row result.
  5. Evaluate Score with field match, LLM-as-judge, or human review — then export findings.
